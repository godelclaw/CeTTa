BioPathNet / PLN lncRNA-target experiment
========================================

Purpose
-------
This is an original matched experiment, not a reproduction claim.  All three
systems are trained afresh on the same frozen splits and five seeds:

  1. the official BioPathNet architecture as a control;
  2. a relation-path PLN evidence model;
  3. a nonnegative logistic hybrid of the two trained models.

Published results are contextual sanity checks only.  Results from this suite
must identify the source commit, dataset hashes, generated run-plan hash, seed,
selected validation checkpoint, and the suite-source fingerprint.  The latter
hashes the executable control, PLN, planning, schema, and compatibility-patch
files so a run plan cannot silently survive a change to experimental code.

The training manifest pins the complete Python package set, BioPathNet and
TorchDrug source commits, the PyTorch/CUDA pair, and two narrow source patches.
The TorchDrug patch updates removed build/header interfaces only; its
SPMM/RSPMM kernels and mathematics are unchanged.  The BioPathNet patch repairs
the relational symmetric scorer: each varying candidate tail receives its own
inverse-direction path computation, evaluated in bounded chunks.  It retains
the configured symmetric average, relation inversion, strict-negative policy,
and biological inputs.  Training refuses an environment whose installed
versions, source files, or patch hash differ.

Data boundary
-------------
train1.txt is the background regulatory graph.  train2.txt supplies supervised
training links and also joins the fact graph, matching the official task.  For
each supervised query, that exact edge and its inverse are removed while paths
are collected.  valid.txt is used only for model and hyperparameter selection.
test.txt is inaccessible to training and selection commands and is evaluated
only through an armed, one-consumption-per-job test plan.

Negative examples are always called "type-matched sampled negatives".  They
are optimization and calibration controls in an incomplete knowledge graph;
they are not assertions that an interaction is biologically false.  Held-out
edges are not consulted while training negatives are sampled.  If fewer than
the requested number of exact-type candidates exist, all available candidates
are used and the shortfall is recorded in the trained artifact.  A singleton
target type contributes positive evidence but no invented negative evidence;
the sampler never broadens the biological target type to fill a quota.

PLN evidence
------------
Path-template positive and sampled-negative counts are learned from train2.
Witness paths sharing their first graph edge form one dependence group.  The
strongest supported template in each group is retained before evidence from
distinct groups is revised.  Every prediction records the paths, template
counts, dependence decision, training manifest, and aggregation rule.

Evaluation
----------
The primary hypothesis is that the hybrid improves query-level MRR over the
freshly trained BioPathNet control.  MR, MRR, Hits@1/3/10 and seed-wise results
are reported.  The primary interval is a paired query bootstrap of the MRR
difference.  Calibration against sampled negatives is reported with that
qualification and is not described as population calibration.

The relation "interact with mRNA" has only two validation examples.  No
relation-stratified split or per-relation tuning is permitted.  Test failures,
missing seeds, and non-improvements are reported rather than discarded.
